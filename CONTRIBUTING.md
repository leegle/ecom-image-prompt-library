# Contributing Guide

## Good Contributions

- Generic e-commerce prompt cases
- Category-specific prompt templates
- Bilingual translations
- Quality checklists
- Anti-collage rules
- Safe compliance wording

## Do Not Submit

- Private brand names
- ASINs
- Seller Central screenshots
- Sales or advertising reports
- Supplier information
- Competitor images or copied competitor layouts
- Misleading claims
- Fake badges or certifications
