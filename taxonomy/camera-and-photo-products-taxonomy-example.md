# Camera & Photo Products Taxonomy Example / 相机与摄影产品 分类树示例

Source: public marketplace category framework and user-provided Best Sellers screenshots.

公开仓库只记录通用分类和产品类型，不保存品牌、ASIN、价格、评论数、卖家工具数据或真实商品图片。

## Level Structure / 层级结构

```text
Amazon Best Sellers
└── Camera & Photo Products / 相机与摄影产品
    ├── Accessories / 摄影配件
    │   └── camera cleaning kit
    │   └── tripod adapter
    ├── Bags & Cases / 摄影包与保护盒
    │   └── camera bag
    │   └── lens case
    ├── Binoculars & Scopes / 双筒望远镜与观测镜
    │   └── compact binoculars
    │   └── spotting scope
    ├── Camcorders / 摄像机
    │   └── portable camcorder
    │   └── video camera
    ├── DSLR Cameras / 单反相机
    │   └── DSLR camera body
    │   └── DSLR kit
    ├── Digital Picture Frames / 数码相框
    │   └── digital photo frame
    │   └── WiFi picture frame
    ├── Lenses / 镜头
    │   └── camera lens
    │   └── macro lens
    ├── Point & Shoot Digital Cameras / 便携数码相机
    │   └── compact camera
    │   └── kids camera
    ├── Surveillance Cameras / 监控摄像头
    │   └── security camera
    │   └── outdoor camera
```

## Compliance Notes / 合规注意

- Do not include private brand names, ASINs, prices, review counts, or copied product images.
- Do not add fake badges, fake certifications, fake ratings, or marketplace logos.
- Do not invent unconfirmed performance claims, dimensions, compatibility, or certifications.
