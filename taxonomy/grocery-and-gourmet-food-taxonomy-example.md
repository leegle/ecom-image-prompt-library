# Grocery & Gourmet Food Taxonomy Example / 食品杂货与美食 分类树示例

Source: public marketplace category framework and user-provided Best Sellers screenshots.

公开仓库只记录通用分类和产品类型，不保存品牌、ASIN、价格、评论数、卖家工具数据或真实商品图片。

```text
Amazon Best Sellers

    ├── Alcoholic Beverages / 酒精饮品
    │   └── craft beverage can pack
    │   └── cocktail mixer bottle
    ├── Baby Foods / 婴儿食品
    │   └── baby puree pouch
    │   └── baby food jar
    ├── Beverages / 饮品
    │   └── energy drink pack
    │   └── protein shake bottle
    ├── Breads & Bakery / 面包与烘焙
    │   └── artisan bread loaf
    │   └── cookie box
    ├── Breakfast Foods / 早餐食品
    │   └── pancake mix box
    │   └── oatmeal cup
    ├── Dairy, Eggs & Plant-Based Alternatives / 乳制品、鸡蛋与植物替代品
    │   └── yogurt pack
    │   └── plant milk carton
    ├── Food & Beverage Gifts / 食品与饮品礼盒
    │   └── gourmet snack box
```
