# Lifestyle Image Prompt / 场景图提示词

```text
Create a premium e-commerce lifestyle image for {product_type}.

Product:
{product_description}

Scene:
{usage_scene}

Requirements:
- Show one realistic use scene only.
- Keep the product accurate and realistic.
- Show the product being used naturally.
- Do not add fake badges, fake certifications, fake ratings, or marketplace logos.
- Do not invent unconfirmed specifications or performance claims.
- Generate one independent image only.
- Do not create collage, grid, 2-panel image, 4-panel image, 6-grid image, 9-grid image, multi-scene overview, split-screen layout, or storyboard sheet.

Style:
Premium commercial product photography, natural soft light, clean composition, realistic material texture.
```
