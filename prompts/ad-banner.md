# Ad Banner Prompt / 广告图提示词

```text
Create a clean e-commerce ad banner for {{product_type}}.

Product:
{{product_description}}

Goal:
{{ad_goal}}

Requirements:
- Show one product and one clear benefit.
- Do not use fake discounts, fake ratings, or marketplace logos.
- Do not use competitor names or logos.
- Do not exaggerate claims.
- Generate one independent banner only.
- Do not create collage, grid, or multi-scene overview.

Style:
Premium commercial ad visual, clean background, strong product focus, minimal text.
```
